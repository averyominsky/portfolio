Context

Rethinking the Museum Visit

Families visiting the ANU Museum on their own may move passively through the exhibits, feel overwhelmed by information, or struggle to connect historical objects to their own lives, especially without a guided tour to provide context. I saw an opportunity to rethink the independent family visit by creating a self-guided interactive “quest” experience that makes the museum more engaging, personal, and meaningful while encouraging families to explore, discuss, and connect with the objects they encounter.

Research

Competitor Analysis

I looked at GooseChase as a reference for location-based scavenger hunts and interactive missions. Its format offered a strong foundation for exploration, but it wasn't built around ANU's collection, family learning, or personal identity.
