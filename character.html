Studio 54:
Culture & Character

I researched Studio 54 and its influence on disco culture in 1970s New York. Inspired by its glamour, energy, and iconic fashion, I used bold silhouettes and expressive styling to capture the spirit of the disco era.

The ensemble

Initially, I explored sketches to determine how to portray the energy and exclusivity of Studio 54. I experimented with poses, spacing, and interactions, ultimately creating three scenes that capture the nightclub experience: the entrance, dance floor, and lounge.

As I refined my images, I developed stronger compositions, clearer details, and more intentional poses inspired by disco fashion and props. I experimented with marker, Micron pens, Sharpie, and Procreate, exploring how line, color, light, and shadow could support the story. I also focused on facial expressions and color contrast to create mood and visual emphasis. Finally, I scanned my sketches and transformed the black linework into pink, allowing me to develop a limited pink and yellow color palette.

Process

Developing the characters and visual language.

I explored poses, compositions, color palettes, and linework before developing the final illustrations. These studies helped establish a consistent visual language across the series.

Research

Looking to Studio 54 for visual inspiration.

I researched Studio 54, disco culture, nightlife photography, fashion, and the visual language surrounding the club. I focused on movement, theatricality, color, and the expressive characters that defined the era.
