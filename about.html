Work Play About

Hi, I'm Avery Ominsky.

I’m primarily interested in the intersection of design, the art market, and technology. I enjoy creating digital experiences through UI/UX and web design, while also incorporating analog storytelling and research. My goal is to bridge communication design with my interests in art history and emerging technology, creating meaningful experiences that connect people with art and culture.

I’m a junior at WashU, where I’m pursuing a BFA in Communication Design with a minor in Business of the Arts. I’ve found my stride developing human-centered design, design research, typography, illustration, branding, and art direction. My skills include Figma, Adobe Creative Suite, Procreate, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. When I’m not working in a Figma file, you can find me drawing, playing mahjong, practicing yoga, or learning Italian.

This summer, I interned with the Education Department at the ANU Museum in Tel Aviv, where I worked on historical research, content development, educational programming, and outreach initiatives. The experience strengthened my interest in using design and storytelling to connect people with art, history, and culture in meaningful ways.
